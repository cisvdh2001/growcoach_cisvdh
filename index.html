Micro-challenges

Small, deliberate actions. One hour a week is enough if you're intentional.

This week · Direct feedback
Give one piece of specific, honest feedback
Pick a colleague and give feedback tied to a real, recent situation. Not "good job" — something like: "When you pushed back on the timeline in that meeting, that was the right call because it protected the delivery date." Specificity is everything. Try the harder version too: feedback that is critical but constructive.
This week · Delegating
Hand off one task you would normally keep
Find something on your plate someone on your support team could handle — even if you would do it faster. Hand it over with clear context: what, why, what good looks like. Do not take it back. Delegation is about scaling your impact, not offloading work.
This month · Visibility
Write a one-page win summary
Document one thing you led or improved recently. Problem, what you did, result. One page max. This becomes raw material for your January conversation. Your manager will not remember everything — you need to remind them.
This month · Communication
Structure a team update with a clear point of view
Next time you update your manager or team, use: situation, what I decided and why, what I need from you. Engineers give status updates. Managers give recommendations. Practice the latter before you have the title.
